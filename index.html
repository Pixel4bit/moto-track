📖 How To Use MotoTrack

  1. 1

    Open Settings, set the Base Odometer based on your current odo.

  2. 2

    In Settings → Components, edit lifespans or add/delete components based on your needs.

  3. 3

    Set the Early Warning System to your preference.

  4. 4

    Don't forget to tap Export to save your current settings and data.

  5. 5

    If you already have saved data, tap Import to load your settings, data, and full service history.

💡 Tip: Export regularly so you never lose your data!
